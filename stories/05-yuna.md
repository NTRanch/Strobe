# Yuna, 24 — "The Face Behind the Screen"

**Anchors:** black hair in a long hime cut worn down; brown eyes;
doll-still composure — a face arranged like a shrine offering, beautiful
and untouchable.

**Backstory.** Yuna is a folklore graduate student writing her thesis on
possession cults, and — under the handle **Miko-9**, face never shown —
one of the most-watched streamers in her category: soft voice, gloved
hands, immaculate desk, a persona her audience calls "pure." Her
boyfriend Ren is her moderator, her editor, and the guardian of that
purity; he loves Miko-9 the way a priest loves a relic and manages Yuna
the way one manages a shrine — carefully, and at arm's length from the
public. What neither the audience nor Ren knows: Yuna poses. Alone, after
streams, she does her makeup like a femme fatale from a 1940s poster and
takes photographs she deletes by morning. She does not want to be a
relic. She wants to be *looked at* — as a woman, hungrily, in the open —
and the wanting has started to feel like the only honest thing about her.

**Hidden trait.** Exhibition of the self she performs in secret: to be
seen as desirable, adult, and dangerous to look at — not "cute," not
"pure."

**The before-bond.** Ren, her boyfriend, whose love is a display case.

**The vector.** Kaoru Shindo, 34, a portrait photographer preparing a
series on "women and their doubles," who books Yuna to consult on shrine
iconography — and recognizes, in the way she arranges a sleeve for his
test frame, a woman who already knows exactly where the light should go.

**The arc.**
- **Stage 0 — Unaware.** Yuna streams faceless, deletes her portraits
  nightly, and lets Ren describe her to sponsors as "wholesome,
  brand-safe, like a little sister."
- **Stage 1 — First Spark.** She books strobe sessions for confidence
  before her thesis defense. In trance the VOICE asks who she is when no
  one is watching, and she whispers: "The woman in the photographs."
- **Stage 2 — Secret Practice.** She stops deleting. A locked folder
  becomes a portfolio. Kaoru's test frames of her — fully clothed,
  arranged like film stills — leave her hands shaking for an hour: she
  has never felt so *seen*, and nothing improper has even happened.
- **Stage 3 — Choosing.** Kaoru proposes the series' centerpiece: Yuna,
  face revealed, as the fox-woman of her own thesis — desired, not cute.
  She says yes before he finishes the sentence, then sits in her car
  shaking at what she's agreed to become.
- **Stage 4 — No More Hiding.** The exhibition opens. Ren sees his
  girlfriend's face ten feet high, looking at the city the way she has
  never been permitted to look at him, and understands he has been
  guarding a door she was starving behind. They part — his grief real,
  her resolve final.
- **Stage 5 — Self-Driven.** Yuna does the face reveal on her own
  channel, on her own terms, in the femme-fatale makeup — and keeps the
  gloves and the soft voice too, because they were always hers. The end
  portrait is the exhibition shot: hime cut gleaming, heart pupils, a
  woman looking at the viewer like *she* is the one doing the looking.

**End state.** Happiest: adored in the open, as the whole woman — relic
and femme fatale in one frame, both of them hers.

**Portrait briefs.**
- *Begin:* hime cut perfect and severe, modest high-collar blouse, eyes
  lowered, composed — the shrine maiden, untouchable.
- *End:* same face; hair glossy and styled, glam makeup, black-and-red
  evening dress, heart pupils, chin up — the fox-woman, looking back.

## Dialogue

*VOICE = the session voice (the player), delivered over the track.*

### Scene 1 — The stream room, after sign-off (Stage 0)

REN: Perfect stream. Chat loved the new gloves. The sponsor deck calls
you "a pure, sisterly presence" — that's our brand, okay? Nothing that
breaks that.
YUNA: Of course. Pure. Sisterly.
REN: You're the best, Yuna. Get some sleep — and no face-cam accidents.
YUNA: *(alone, opening the makeup drawer)* No accidents. *(beat)* Only
rituals.

### Scene 2 — First session (Stage 1)

VOICE: The beat is a metronome for the truth. Who are you when no one is
watching?
YUNA: A student. A streamer. Ren's—
VOICE: When *no one* is watching.
YUNA: *(whispering)* The woman in the photographs. The ones I delete.
VOICE: Then don't delete tonight. Let her live until morning. That's the
only instruction.
YUNA: …She'd like that. She'd like that too much.

### Scene 3 — Kaoru's studio, test frames (Stage 2)

KAORU: Hold the sleeve— no. You already know. Don't perform modesty for
my camera, Yuna; it can tell.
YUNA: Everyone can tell everything, apparently, except the people who
know me.
KAORU: Then they're not looking at you. They're looking at the frame
they built. *(shutter)* I don't build frames. I remove them.
YUNA: *(barely audible)* Then remove carefully. There's a lot behind
this one.

### Scene 4 — The proposal (Stage 3)

KAORU: The series needs its center. The fox-woman. Face revealed. Not
cute, Yuna — *wanted*. It will change how everyone sees you. It will
change how *he*—
YUNA: Yes.
KAORU: I haven't finished—
YUNA: I know what it costs. I've been paying the other price for years.
Yes.

### Scene 5 — The exhibition (Stage 4)

REN: Ten feet tall. My girlfriend, looking at strangers like— Yuna, we
had a *brand*. We had a whole life built on—
YUNA: On me staying behind glass. Ren, look at the photograph. Really
look. Have you ever seen me that alive?
REN: *(long silence)* No. That's the worst part. No.
YUNA: Then let me go be alive. Please. You loved the relic beautifully.
Let the woman go.

### Scene 6 — The face reveal, her channel (Stage 5)

YUNA: *(to camera, face revealed, radiant)* Good evening. You've known
my voice for three years. Tonight you get the rest of me — all of it was
always me. The gloves stay. The soft voice stays. And so does *she* —
the one you've been seeing in the exhibition prints. We're the same
girl. We always were. *(smiling)* Now. Shall we begin?

# Identity Anchors — the Strobe Cast

**Differentiation rules (ruled 2026-10-19): the Strobe girls carry
different names *and* different looks from their Light Show counterparts.**
Same archetype slots, different women. Every heroine below differs from
her Light Show namesake in name, hair color and/or cut, signature
accessories, and register. Light Show's girls are telecom field trainees —
fiber-optic hair accessories, light-pipe visors, RJ45 pins, patch-cable
braids, LUMENET badges. None of that exists here. The Strobe cast are
adult women in civilian lives, and their signatures come from their
professions and desires.

Anchors are invariant *within* Strobe (rule D1): the same face, natural
eye color, and hair color at Stage 0 and Stage 5. Transformation is
carried by styling, hair *arrangement* (never color), makeup, wardrobe,
expression, and bearing. End-state portraits add the game's signature
heart pupils; natural iris color always shows through.

# STROBE

**Ten women. Ten hidden desires. One phone that knows.**

A strobe light catches what continuous light hides — motion, frame by
frame, in flashes too honest to pose for. *Strobe* is an adult game
about those flashes: the moment a woman's performance slips, and the
desire she's been hiding shows through.

Every heroine in *Strobe* is living a double life. Not a lie — a
*performance*: the good girlfriend, the competent professional, the
woman who has it handled. Underneath, each of them wants something
specific, kinky, and entirely her own. She has always wanted it. Your
job is not to change her. Your job is to be the one she's finally
honest with.

This is a sex-positive game about kinky women taking their own desire
seriously — built so that going deeper is always her idea as much as
yours.

## The fantasy

Her boyfriend loves the woman she performs. You'll meet the woman she
is.

Each arc follows one heroine through six stages — from *Unaware*,
burying it even from herself, through the first spark and the secret
practice, to the flip: the moment she stops hiding and starts
*choosing*. From there on, every step deeper is hers, taken with open
eyes. Nobody is broken in this game, and nobody is ruined. The endings
are the happiest states these women reach — by their own measure.

Progress is measured in **Corruption**: her growing appetite for her
own kink. It rises only when play matches what *she* actually wants —
learn her, and she opens like a lock turning. Rush her, play off her
desire, and it cools. There is no way to install a desire she doesn't
have. There is only the one she's been waiting to be caught having.

## How it plays

Everything runs through one in-fiction phone — six apps, no more:

- **Status** — where she is: her stage, her baselines, and her
  *frequency* — the learnable sweet spot where her kink sings.
- **Session** — the encounter. Two bars: **Response**, how deep she
  is right now, and **Corruption**, how far her desire has grown.
  Play to her frequency and both climb.
- **Store** — purchases are new verbs, never stat sticks. What you
  buy changes what you can *do*.
- **Protocols** — her capstones arrive as app upgrades, each with her
  reaction attached. She notices what she's becoming.
- **Feed** — check on her between sessions. Her face, her idle
  moments, her stage — she changes on every surface.
- **Aftercare** — every session closes here. Not a reward screen. A
  ritual.

Her desire levels along four **Response tracks — Voice, Gaze, Touch,
Rhythm** — each topping out in a capstone named in her own language
for her own kink. Capstones are keys: new places, new verbs, deeper
tiers. Each heroine's story plays as a **season** on a bounded
campaign clock — a few choices a day, an ending that's a soft landing,
never a game-over wall.

And when she reaches **Craving**, she starts initiating sessions
herself. At **Self-Driven**, she requests them in advance. By the end,
you're not pursuing her. You're keeping up.

## Safe by design, deep by choice

Safety in *Strobe* is not a warning label. It's the machinery that
makes depth possible:

- **Her comfort limit is a hard stop, not a fail state.** Hit it and
  the session ends gracefully — no progress lost, no punishment — and
  moves straight to aftercare.
- **Aftercare closes every session.** Always. It's part of the loop,
  not an unlock.
- **She escalates.** Deeper tiers unlock at her Corruption thresholds
  and are signaled by her. The game's vocabulary for this is consent,
  all the way down.
- **Nothing is installed.** Corruption only ever grows along the
  desire she already has.

## The hardware lane — strictly opt-in

*Strobe* can drive a Lovense toy in time with play — and it is built
like it matters:

- **Off by default.** The master switch ships OFF. Nothing connects,
  nothing moves, until you turn it on.
- **Hard ceilings.** Output is clamped to the lowest of your personal
  ceiling, the tier her Corruption has actually unlocked, and an
  absolute cap of 20 — the hardware never outruns her story.
- **Stop means stop.** Stopping, disarming, or hitting her comfort
  limit zeroes output synchronously, in the same instant — watched by
  a 5-second watchdog that kills output if the link so much as
  stumbles.
- **No surprises.** If the connection drops, output never resumes on
  reconnect. Ever.

When it's on, the sync is two-layered: a **music layer** that pulses
with the track's rhythm and intensity whether you play well or not,
and a **judgement layer** where your timing becomes touch — dead-center
hits land full, edge hits land faint, and even a miss has a texture:
one short, dull, low thud. She feels how centered you are.

## Under the hood

*Strobe* is built in **Rust on the Bevy engine**, with deterministic,
rig-based animation — real models, authored motion curves, rendered
frames — because desire should move like it means it, the same way
every time. The design's logic core was proven first (82 automated
tests, green) and is being ported to Rust against those tests as its
specification.

**Status: in active development.** The cast book is written, all
twenty portraits are done, the vertical slice — one heroine, end to
end — is in production, and the full design record lives in this repo:
the [game book](book/) renders the cast, tracker, and decisions as a
readable site. Release route: itch.io first.
